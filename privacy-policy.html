Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

CHRGD is an EV charging network operated by Solargy e-Mobility (a subsidiary of Solargy Group). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the CHRGD app or our charging stations, why we collect it, and how we use and protect it.

1. Data We Collect

When you create a CHRGD account or use our app and charging stations, we may collect:

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number.
  • Authentication data: One-time passcodes (OTP) delivered via WhatsApp through our integration with Pivotal messaging services.
  • Location data: Your approximate or precise location (with permission) to show nearby stations and navigate you to them.
  • Wallet and payment data: Top-up amounts, transaction history, and payment method tokens (we do not store full card numbers — payments are processed by a third-party payment gateway).
  • Charging session data: Station ID, connector used, session start/end times, energy delivered (kWh), and cost.
  • Device and usage data: Device type, operating system, app version, and usage logs for diagnostic purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate your CHRGD account and authenticate your identity.
  • To process charging sessions, deduct wallet credit, and generate invoices.
  • To control Parklio smart parking barriers associated with your session.
  • To send you session notifications, wallet alerts, and service communications.
  • To improve the reliability and performance of our network and app.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Third-Party Services

We work with the following third-party services in delivering the CHRGD platform:

  • Payment gateway: Card top-ups and transactions are processed by a PCI-DSS compliant payment provider. We do not store raw card data.
  • Pivotal / WhatsApp OTP: Phone number verification and one-time passcodes are delivered via Pivotal's WhatsApp messaging integration. Your phone number is shared with Pivotal for this purpose.
  • Parklio: To unlock smart parking barriers, we send a command to Parklio's API linked to your session. Minimal session data is shared for this purpose.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with sub-processors required to deliver the service (as listed above), or where required by law or regulatory authority.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including TLS encryption in transit and access controls. No system is perfectly secure — please contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [insert contact email].

7. Data Retention

We retain account and session data for as long as your account is active and for a period afterwards as required by law or our legitimate interests. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.
