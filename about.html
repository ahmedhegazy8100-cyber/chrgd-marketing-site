About CHRGD

We run the network, so you don't have to think about it

CHRGD is an independent electric vehicle charging network provider. We select the sites, install high-power hardware, set fair tariffs, and keep everything running — so when you plug in, it just works.

Backed by nearly 10 years of experience in e-mobility charging solutions, CHRGD brings deep expertise in charging infrastructure and power management. CHRGD is our answer to what public EV charging should look like: accessible, transparent, and built for the long run.

Mission & Values

Why we do this

Make charging effortless

Every decision we make is filtered through one question: does this make it easier to charge? If not, we change it.

Be honest about where we are

We're a growing network — not a global brand (yet). We'll always be straight with you about availability, pricing, and what to expect.

Protect your time

We focus on high-speed hardware and reliable station availability so charging fits seamlessly into your day. Your time matters.

Build for the long term

EV infrastructure is infrastructure. We invest in quality hardware and active monitoring so stations stay online, not just looking good on paper.

How it started

From energy expertise to EV infrastructure

With nearly 10 years of experience in e-mobility charging solutions, CHRGD was created to solve the real-world challenges of public EV charging — where stations were often unreliable, pricing was opaque, and quality was an afterthought.

So we built CHRGD as a dedicated, standalone charging network that we own and operate ourselves — accountable to drivers every single day.

We're still early in that journey. Our network is growing across Egypt, our app is coming, and we're just getting started.
