About CHRGD

We run the network, so you don't have to think about it

CHRGD is Solargy e-Mobility's own electric vehicle charging network. We select the sites, install the hardware, set the tariffs, and keep everything running — so when you plug in, it just works.

Solargy e-Mobility is a subsidiary of Solargy Group, which brings deep roots in renewable energy, grid intelligence, and sustainable infrastructure. CHRGD is our answer to what public EV charging should look like: accessible, transparent, and built for the long run.

Mission & Values

Why we do this

Make charging effortless

Every decision we make is filtered through one question: does this make it easier to charge? If not, we change it.

Be honest about where we are

We're a growing network — not a global brand (yet). We'll always be straight with you about availability, pricing, and what to expect.

Protect your time

Smart parking barriers exist so your bay is waiting when you arrive — not blocked by a petrol car. Your time matters.

Build for the long term

EV infrastructure is infrastructure. We invest in quality hardware and active monitoring so stations stay online, not just looking good on paper.

How it started

From energy expertise to EV infrastructure

Solargy Group has spent years working at the intersection of clean energy and intelligent grid systems. As electric vehicles went from niche to mainstream, it became clear that the charging experience wasn't keeping pace — stations were unreliable, pricing was opaque, and parking protection was an afterthought.

So we built CHRGD. Not as a product to sell to others, but as a network we run ourselves — one we're accountable for every day. Solargy e-Mobility was created to focus on exactly this: owning and operating a charging network that people can actually trust.

We're still early in that journey. Our network is growing, our app is coming, and we're just getting started.
