Step-by-Step

Charging is easier than you think

Four steps. No fuss. No complicated buttons or special cards. Just your phone and your cable.

1

Open the app and check your wallet

Before you head out, open the CHRGD app to see your wallet balance and locate the nearest available station on the map. Top up if needed — fast, in-app.

[media]
Illustration: app open on station finder screen with wallet balance visible.
2

Unlock the parking barrier at your station

When you arrive, tap your reserved bay in the app. The Parklio smart barrier lowers automatically — no warden, no ticket, no stress. Your space is ready.

[media]
Illustration: smart parking barrier lowering, EV car pulling into bay.
3

Plug in — charging starts automatically

Connect your cable. That's it. Charging begins straight away — no button to press on the charger, no RFID card to tap. The app tracks everything live.

[media]
Illustration: cable plugging into CHRGD charge point, active charging indicator.
4

End your session in the app when done

When you're ready to go, tap "End session" in the app. Your invoice is generated instantly. Unplug, drive off — and the barrier returns to protect the bay for the next driver.

[media]
Illustration: session complete screen showing kWh delivered and cost summary.

Ready to give it a go?

Download the CHRGD app to find a station, top up your wallet, and start charging. Available soon on iOS and Android.
