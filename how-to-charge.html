Step-by-Step

Charging is easier than you think

Three steps. No fuss. No complicated buttons or special cards. Just your vehicle and your cable.

1

Locate your nearest station

Find the nearest available CHRGD station across Egypt on our map or inside the CHRGD app.

2

Plug in — charging starts automatically

Connect your cable. That's it. Charging begins straight away — no button to press on the charger, no RFID card to tap.

3

End your session when done

When you're ready to go, tap "End session" in the app or unplug your vehicle. Your invoice is generated instantly. Unplug, drive off, and you're on your way.

Premium Commercial Hardware

Every CHRGD station is equipped with high-power Schneider Electric & DS 90 charging posts, smart LED status rings, integrated cable management, and weatherproof illuminated canopies for 24/7 charging safety.
