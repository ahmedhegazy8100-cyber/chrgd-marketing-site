Transparent Pricing

Pay as you go. No surprises.

Charging with CHRGD is straightforward — load your wallet, use what you need, and see exactly what you spend. No monthly fees. No contracts.

How it works

Simple, wallet-based charging

Top up your wallet

Add credit to your CHRGD in-app wallet using a debit or credit card. Top up as little or as much as you like — you're always in control.

Pay per session

Your wallet is charged based on the energy you use, priced per kWh. The current rate is shown in the app at every station before you start. Check the app for the latest [rate] at your local station.

Your receipt, instantly

As soon as your session ends, you'll get a full invoice in the app — kWh delivered, duration, and total cost. Clear, honest, and always on hand.

Idle time fee

If your vehicle is fully charged but still connected, a small idle fee may apply after a grace period — this keeps bays available for other drivers. The fee and grace period are always shown in the app at that station.

Prices may vary by station and are always shown clearly before you start a session. The most current pricing is available in the CHRGD app.

Common questions

Pricing FAQs

Do I need a subscription?

No. CHRGD is pay-as-you-go. Just top up your wallet and charge when you need to — there are no monthly fees or contracts.

Can I get a refund on my wallet balance?

Yes. Unspent wallet balance can be refunded to your original payment method. See the Terms of Service for details.

Is there a minimum top-up?

The minimum wallet top-up amount is shown in the app. It's designed to be low enough to cover a single charging session.

Are prices different at different stations?

Rates are shown per station in the app before you start. We aim to keep pricing consistent and transparent across the network.

See live pricing in the app

Current rates for every station, your wallet balance, and full session history — all in one place.
