What the app does

Built around how you actually charge

Find a station

Browse an interactive map of CHRGD stations near you. See real-time bay availability, connector type, and pricing before you drive over.

Unlock smart parking

Tap your reserved bay in the app to lower the Parklio barrier automatically. Your space is protected from the moment you book it.

Start & monitor your charge

Plug in and the session begins. Watch your kWh, charge speed, and cost update live — so you always know what you're getting.

Top up your wallet

Add credit with a debit or credit card in seconds. Your wallet balance is always visible at the top of the app.

Invoices on demand

Every session generates an instant invoice in the app — itemised, shareable, and saved in your history for as long as you need it.

Account & notifications

Get notified when your charge is complete, when your wallet is running low, or when new stations open near you.
